Chocolate Chip Cookies

Description

A delicious chewy chocolate chip cookies recipe.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 3/4 cup unsalted butter, melted
  • 1 large egg
  • 1 egg yolk
  • 1 tablespoon vanilla extract
  • 2 cups semisweet chocolate chips

Steps

  1. Gather all ingredients. Preheat the oven to 325 degrees F (165 degrees C). Grease cookie sheets or line with parchment paper
  2. Sift flour, baking soda, and salt together; set aside.

  4. Stir in chocolate chips.
  5. Drop spoonfuls of dough 3 inches apart onto the prepared baking sheets.
  6. Bake cookies in the preheated oven until edges are golden, about 15 to 17 minutes.
  7. Cool on the cookie sheets briefly before transferring them to a wire rack to cool completely.
